# Localytics

> Mobile analytics and engagement platform. Localytics reads the signals your app is already producing and turns them into personalized campaigns — push, in-app, and inbox messaging — that reach each customer when it matters most.

Localytics helps product, marketing, and engineering teams understand mobile app users and engage them through targeted, data-driven campaigns across push notifications, in-app messages, and inbox messaging.
